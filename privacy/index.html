Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

ProductBeacon is run by Etsion Brands Ltd, a company registered in Israel, Meitav 5, Tel Aviv 6789804, Israel. That company decides how your personal data is used.

Questions and requests: hello@productbeacon.agency

This site is for working professionals aged 18 or over.

In short

  • We collect what you send us, and basic data about your visit.
  • We use it to answer you, to handle an order, and to see which pages people read.
  • We do not sell or rent it.
  • You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time.

When you write to us or order a Market Report

“Order a private Market Report” opens an email in your own mail program. We receive what you write: your address, your name if you sign, your organisation and your question.

We use it to reply, to check that your question fits our rules, and, if you order, to agree terms, deliver the report and the call, and invoice you. We keep a short private record of each inquiry: your organisation, the role of the person who would sign, and our checks. It holds no name and no email address.

Mail to our addresses passes through Cloudflare and rests in our Microsoft 365 mailbox, kept in Israel at present. Yohay Etsion reads it with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, made by Anthropic). The text of your message goes to Anthropic in the United States, which keeps it for 30 days and does not use it to train its models. Our replies go out through Resend, from yohay@productbeacon.agency.

We keep your order and your question private, except that we name a paying vendor, as our terms say. If you give us feedback, we may note the point in our own words, without your name.

Our ground: you asked us to, and we need the data to agree and carry out your order. Invoices: tax law.

Our forms

  • Contact form. Company, name, email, website, one choice and your message. Formspree (United States) processes it and emails it to our mailbox.
  • Operator Briefing registration and Intensive waiting list. Name, email, an optional LinkedIn address and one answer. Web3Forms processes them, adds your IP address, and may pass your IP and email address to two spam checks, CleanTalk and Akismet. It emails the details to our mailbox.

We use form details to answer you, register you or hold your place, because you asked us to.

When you visit

  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the site, and counts page visits with a small script that sets no cookie. GitHub Pages hosts the site. Both see your IP address, your browser details and the page you ask for.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load their fonts from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Google Analytics shows us which pages are read and which buttons are pressed, including the order buttons (which button, on which page). It sets two cookies, _ga and _ga_TC1LMMGQGV, that last over a year. Google signals, which links visits to Google accounts, is off. To refuse it, block cookies for this site in your browser.
  • A few pages we share by direct link show another company’s logo straight from that company’s website, which then sees your IP address.
  • Notes you write in the margin of a report page stay in your own browser. We never receive them.

Our ground: our legitimate interest in a site that works, stays safe and is read.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, or stop or limit a use of it. Write to hello@productbeacon.agency from the address the request is about. It is free, and we answer within one month. Some rights depend on where you live; if a limit applies, we will tell you.

Please tell us first if something is wrong, so we can put it right. You may also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work: in the European Union, your national authority; in the United Kingdom, the Information Commission; in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority.

You are under no legal duty to give us any data. Without it, we cannot reply or take your order. We make no decision about you by automated means alone.
