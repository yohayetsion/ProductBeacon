PROFESSION-GROUNDED ROLES

Every specialist has a profession before it has a task.

A generic agent starts with the prompt in front of it. A ProductBeacon specialist starts with a defined organisational role: what it is accountable for, how it works, which methods it can use, what professional knowledge it can draw on, and where its boundaries sit. The task then determines which role runs and which skills, knowledge, and company context it receives.

ProductBeacon specialist

01Identity

The role, accountability, working style, and handoff expectations stay consistent across runs.

02Remit

The organisational responsibility and work this role owns are defined before the task arrives.

03Skills

Repeatable procedures tell the specialist how to produce a specific piece of work.

04Professional knowledge

Role-relevant frameworks, patterns, and reference material are loaded when the job needs them.

05Boundaries

Sensitive work carries triggered controls, human handoffs, and explicit limits on what the specialist may decide.

More than a task prompt.

A role is more than a name. Identity, procedures, professional knowledge, context, and boundaries arrive together.

A narrow request can stay with one specialist. Cross-functional work can use separate specialist runs and one synthesis owner before human review.

The know-how is part of the role.

The current workforce carries over 300 ready-made skills and knowledge libraries. The operator does not need to choose from a catalogue. The harness selects what the specialist needs for the assigned work.

A GOVERNED SPAWN

Every run is a separate, governed process.

ProductBeacon does not pour the whole company into one chat. Each specialist is started for one assigned piece of work, with a specific identity and a focused context. Cross-functional work can use several separate specialist runs, then one synthesis owner brings the work together.

  1. 01You frame the outcome
  2. 02Relevant context is found
  3. 03The role is established
  4. 04Skills and knowledge are selected
  5. 05Boundaries and controls are applied
  6. 06The specialist does the work
  7. 07Output and Spawn Audit Block are returned

The run receives only the context selected for its job. When prior decisions or sensitive controls apply, they are brought into the run before execution.

A named human reviews the handoff.The run prepares and declares work; it does not take the consequential decision.

FULL HARNESS

The model is only one part of the worker.

Full Harness is what surrounds the model with a professional role, methods, knowledge, company context, governing instructions, triggered controls, and an auditable handoff. It is how a plausible answer becomes accountable work you can review.

A run receipt, not a correctness certificate.

The Spawn Audit Block records what the specialist declared it loaded and produced. It does not expose private reasoning, certify the truth of the answer, or replace human review.

THE HUMAN DECISION SEAM

AI can prepare the decision. A named human owns it.

The Decision Provenance Standard keeps the line visible. It connects AI-supported analysis to the accountable human through records that can be reviewed, affirmed, audited, and resumed. An agent may draft a Decision Record. Only the named accountable owner can affirm it closed.

Read the Decision Provenance Standard

  1. Evidence and analysis prepared
  2. Decision Record drafted
  3. Pending human affirmation
  4. Human accepts, challenges, or changes it
  5. Closed as an affirmable, resumable record

The agent proposes. The named human decides. The record preserves which happened.

LOOP AND LEARN

The work is planned, checked, reviewed, and remembered.

You frame what good looks like. The workforce plans and executes in stages, reviewers with a fresh brief test the result, and you make the consequential calls. What is worth carrying forward is recorded so a later run can start with more of the right context.

AUDITA fresh review checks the plan or result against its contract.
HUMAN REVIEWYou accept, challenge, change, or send back.
RECORDUseful context is kept for later work.

DISTRIBUTED COMPANY BRAIN

The company brain is the records between the runs.

Decisions, strategic bets, assumptions, feedback, learnings, and documents are kept as typed, inspectable records. Before a specialist runs, the harness searches those sources and selects what is relevant to that job. The specialist does not see everything, and the memory can be corrected when reality changes.

Distributed Company Brain is a metaphor for shared, inspectable records and selective recall. It is not consciousness, perfect memory, universal access, or a claim that every source is current.
