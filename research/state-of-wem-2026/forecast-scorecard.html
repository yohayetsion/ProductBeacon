How to read a forecast

  1. Forecast, as published. Copied word for word from the report.
  2. Deadline as stated. The date the forecast gives, if any.
  3. Verdict. Held, Failed, Open or Void, as defined below.
  4. Evidence so far. Our published weekly radar's words, if any.

  • Held

    what the forecast said would confirm it happened inside its window.

  • Failed

    what it said would weaken it happened, or the window ended without the confirming outcome.

  • Open

    neither yet.

  • Void

    the test can no longer happen.

Notes on method

For anyone who wants to check our work.

Our rules for verdicts

  • A missed deadline counts as Failed. A forecast with no date stays Open until one of its outcomes is met on a dated public source.
  • We read "by Q4 2026", "H2 2026" and "through 2026" as by 31 December 2026; where a forecast names earnings calls, it waits for the calls about the quarters it names.
  • A deal announced before a report was published does not count toward that report's forecasts, even if it completes later.
  • A forecast that says a company is acquired or absorbed counts only when the deal completes; a forecast about how a deal is framed counts when the deal is announced.
  • Every verdict other than Open names its public source and date.
  • Each verdict is our reading of the public sources it names.
  • Evidence so far is the radar's own words, quoted from a published radar brief.
  • The full State of WEM 2026 report prints eight of these forecasts with small editing changes, mostly punctuation and quote marks, and in one case "headline QM marketing copy" for "QM hero-messaging". We quote the chapter page, which each row links to. No edit changes a date, a number or a named company.
  • To report an error of fact, write to yohay@productbeacon.agency with the public source.
