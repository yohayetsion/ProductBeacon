Emerging AI Theater

Failed

In plain words: AI-security start-ups would keep being bought by platforms, not going public or raising $100 million or more in one round.

Why it failed: HiddenLayer, an independent AI-security company, announced a $100 million round on 2 September 2026: the forecast's own sign of failure.

Sources: HiddenLayer, 2 September 2026

Pattern Claim 1: The Pre-Gravity Window Thesis

Forecast, as published:

  • Watch the next two-to-four quarters for a fourth named AI-application-security or AI-SecOps independent absorbed into a platform incumbent in named-outlet M&A coverage. If the next notable AI-security exits are again acquisitions of that kind, the pre-Gravity window stays open only as a way station, and absorption is confirmed as the category's default exit.
  • The window's close would be marked instead by the inverse signal: the first pure-play AI-security acquisition above ~$200M as a standalone valuation, or the first AI-security pure-play to cross to a public listing or a disclosed $100M-plus standalone private round. Either of those, in named-outlet M&A coverage, SEC filings, or a dated funding announcement, would mark a pure-play graduating to Gravity rather than being absorbed below it.
  • I am grounding this on the public M&A and funding record, not on any regulatory date.

Deadline as stated: “the next two-to-four quarters”

The full reason: The forecast named a disclosed $100M-plus standalone private round by an AI-security pure-play as the sign that the window had closed, and HiddenLayer, the independent the chapter singles out, announced a $100 million Series B on 2 September 2026. This reads "$100M-plus" as $100 million or more and "the first" as the first after publication, because Noma Security's $100 million Series B was announced on 31 July 2025, before the report.

Other sources: SiliconANGLE, 2025-07-31; SecurityWeek, 2026-07-20

Held

Why it held: Okta framed its Permiso deal around "human, non-human, and agentic identities"; IBM framed its AuthMind deal around AI agents.

Sources: Okta, 30 July 2026; IBM, 12 November 2026

Cross-Front Pattern Claims, claim 1

Forecast, as published:

  • The single-contest thesis validates as the default resolution — and the four fronts are confirmed as one platform war — if the next one or two notable platform identity or AI acquisitions are again framed around agent / machine / non-human identity in the acquirers' own dated press and named-outlet M&A coverage. The clearest such signal would be a fourth platform giant beyond Palo Alto, CrowdStrike, Cisco, and Microsoft buying identity on an agent rationale.
  • The vector weakens, and the fronts read as adjacent-but-separate consolidations, if instead the M&A rationale reverts to classic SSO, undifferentiated gap-filling, or pricing — or if a workforce-IAM pure-play reaches genuine disclosed scale and scales independently with no platform absorption.
  • I am grounding every threshold on the dated M&A record and earnings disclosure — observable public signals an outsider can watch — not on any regulation's timing.

Deadline as stated: No date stated

The full reason: The next notable platform identity deal after the forecast's publication on 21 June 2026, Okta's purchase of Permiso (announced 30 July, completed 26 August 2026), was framed around threats across "human, non-human, and agentic identities". IBM, a platform company beyond the four the forecast names, announced on 12 November 2026 an acquisition of AuthMind framed around AI agents, the forecast's clearest signal; the test is about how deals are framed, so the verdict does not depend on whether IBM's deal has closed.

How to read a forecast

  1. Forecast, as published. Copied word for word from the report.
  2. Deadline as stated. The date the forecast gives, if any.
  3. Verdict. Held, Failed, Open or Void, as defined below.
  4. Evidence so far. Our published weekly radar's words, if any.

  • Held

    what the forecast said would confirm it happened inside its window.

  • Failed

    what it said would weaken it happened, or the window ended without the confirming outcome.

  • Open

    neither yet.

  • Void

    the test can no longer happen.
