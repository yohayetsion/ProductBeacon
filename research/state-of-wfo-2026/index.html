Update 02 · August 12, 2026 · Weekly radar

Three Vendors Put a Number on AI

Five9, NiCE and Genesys each disclosed an artificial intelligence revenue line within one fortnight: 15% of subscription revenue, 15% of cloud revenue, and about 20% of new business contract value. The denominators differ, so these are not one measurement. What they share is that all three now treat the AI line as material enough to disclose. The counter-evidence held: Gartner found 20% of organisations cut agent headcount because of AI.
