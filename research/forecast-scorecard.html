How we score

  • Held: what the forecast said would confirm it happened inside its window.
  • Failed: what it said would weaken it happened, or the window ended without the confirming outcome.
  • Open: neither yet.
  • Void: the test can no longer happen.

A missed deadline counts as Failed. A forecast with no date stays Open until one of its outcomes is met on a dated public source.

We read "by Q4 2026", "H2 2026" and "through 2026" as by 31 December 2026; where a forecast names earnings calls, it waits for the calls about the quarters it names.

A deal announced before a report was published does not count toward that report's forecasts, even if it completes later.

A forecast that says a company is acquired or absorbed counts only when the deal completes; a forecast about how a deal is framed counts when the deal is announced.

Every verdict other than Open names its public source and date.

Each verdict is our reading of the public sources it names.

Evidence so far is the radar's own words, quoted from a published radar brief.

The Cyber Radar also tracks one prediction that matches no forecast on this page, "AI-native challengers keep a distinctive edge in security operations", which its brief of 24 October 2026 rates "Weakened (unchanged)".

State of Cyber 2026 · Emerging AI Theater · Pattern Claim 1: The Pre-Gravity Window Thesis

Forecast, as published:

  • Watch the next two-to-four quarters for a fourth named AI-application-security or AI-SecOps independent absorbed into a platform incumbent in named-outlet M&A coverage. If the next notable AI-security exits are again acquisitions of that kind, the pre-Gravity window stays open only as a way station, and absorption is confirmed as the category's default exit.
  • The window's close would be marked instead by the inverse signal: the first pure-play AI-security acquisition above ~$200M as a standalone valuation, or the first AI-security pure-play to cross to a public listing or a disclosed $100M-plus standalone private round. Either of those, in named-outlet M&A coverage, SEC filings, or a dated funding announcement, would mark a pure-play graduating to Gravity rather than being absorbed below it.
  • I am grounding this on the public M&A and funding record, not on any regulatory date.

Deadline as stated: “the next two-to-four quarters”

Verdict on 17 Nov 2026: Failed. The forecast named a disclosed $100M-plus standalone private round by an AI-security pure-play as the sign that the window had closed, and HiddenLayer, the independent the chapter singles out, announced a $100 million Series B on 2 September 2026. This reads "$100M-plus" as $100 million or more and "the first" as the first after publication, because Noma Security's $100 million Series B was announced on 31 July 2025, before the report.

Sources: HiddenLayer, 2026-09-02; SiliconANGLE, 2025-07-31; SecurityWeek, 2026-07-20

Evidence so far, in the radar's own words: No published radar brief assesses this forecast yet.

  • Force-test (vs the null — two unrelated arcs): if the next cross-layer move by a shared incumbent — or by a fourth platform giant beyond Microsoft / PANW / CrowdStrike — carries ONE agent-governance rationale spanning data AND identity in dated acquirer press, the shared force is confirmed and the coincidental-scale null falsifies.
