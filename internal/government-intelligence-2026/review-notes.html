This is the C6 internal review pack for The State of Government and Law Enforcement Intelligence Markets 2026. It includes the reader repairs, a dedicated Collateral map and a separate Report Digest. Hosting this pack does not approve public publication.

C5-1: Each market now has the correct tier legend

Earlier issue: one shared legend used “Wildcard” in Fusion and Open-Source Intelligence, where the third tier is “Challenger”.

C6 disposition: the first three market decks retain Wildcard; Fusion and Open-Source Intelligence use their own Challenger explanations. Convergence explicitly explains the mixed labels. No vendor was regraded.

Evidence: the maintained per-market legend mapping, rebuilt decks and C6 legend regression checks.

C5-2: PDF tags are added; a viewer limitation remains

Earlier issue: the eight PDFs lacked tagged document structure and diagram alternatives.

C6 disposition: all nine current PDFs, including the new Digest, have tagged headings, lists and tables, document language, link annotations and source-bound figure alternatives. Checks compare decoded reading order, table row/cell order and header scope with the document source. Print ligatures and split footnote labels were corrected where they damaged extracted text. Deliberately damaged tags, alternatives, reading order and links are detected.

Remaining PDF-reader issue: Chromium's built-in PDF viewer exposes text and links in the tested Digest, Pre-Call and Open-Source Intelligence files, but flattens heading levels and does not expose the table associations or diagram alternatives present in the PDFs. Tags in a file do not establish usable support in that viewer. This limitation remains open; it is not labelled repaired or treated as a full accessibility pass.

Reading alternative: the full report on the web, Pre-Call and Digest remain available. The actual browser accessibility interface preserves the web report's heading levels, table/header/cell relationships and all 30 diagram titles/descriptions; the Pre-Call table is also exposed. This is tested interface behavior, not a blanket accessibility certification.

Owner disposition, September 13, 2026: the owner accepts this documented PDF-viewer limitation and declines further human assistive-reader testing as a prerequisite for this draft or release. The limitation remains; no human screen-reader session or PDF/UA certification has been completed. The HTML readers remain the available alternative.

Evidence: C6 per-PDF semantic records and damaged-PDF tests; the independent QA report's actual viewer probes; c6-main-html-accessibility-2026-09-13.json. The plan explicitly permits this named viewer constraint and concrete remedy; it does not permit an unqualified accessible-PDF claim.

C5-3: The older Palantir quarter is explicitly historical

Earlier issue: a Fusion sentence presented the Q1 annualised calculation and 76% growth in the present tense alongside newer Q2 figures.

C6 disposition: the approved sentence now begins “In Q1 FY2026” and uses past tense. Its USD 3.4 billion calculation, USD 858 million quarter and 76% observation are preserved. The newer Q2 Government-segment figures remain distinct.

Evidence: the exact approved source-patch receipt and rebuilt Fusion/full-report consumers. The Digest separately preserves the Government versus U.S. Government denominator distinction and links both accepted source documents.

C5-4: The vendor grouping no longer looks like a formal grade

Earlier issue: “Challengers (Siren, DataWalk, i2, Nuix)” suggested that all four vendors shared a formal tier.

C6 disposition: the approved wording is “Competing vendors”. Siren remains Challenger; the grading table and all other vendor grades are unchanged.

Evidence: the exact approved source-patch receipt and current Fusion consumers.
