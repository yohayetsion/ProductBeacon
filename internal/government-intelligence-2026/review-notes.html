Review notes

This is the September review pack for The State of Government and Law Enforcement Intelligence Markets 2026. Review the report as it would appear on ProductBeacon, with the known limitations below alongside it. Hosting this pack does not approve publication.

Research-page preview · Report home · Full report

These twelve items came forward from the completed report refresh. Listing them here does not authorise all twelve repairs. The six source chapters remain frozen. The two wording issues in the Fusion chapter require a specific owner decision before that boundary changes.

C5-1: The shared legend uses the wrong tier name in two markets

Reader impact: the shared legend says “Wildcard” where the Fusion and Open-Source Intelligence markets use “Challenger”. The same legend is correct in the first three markets.

Disposition: retained under the owner's ruling. A future change must give each market its appropriate legend; changing the shared word everywhere would create new errors.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-1; assembly/legend-box-copy.md and its six deck consumers.

C5-2: The PDFs are not tagged for accessibility

Reader impact: the eight PDFs lack document structure and alternative descriptions for diagrams needed by assistive technology. This pack does not describe them as accessible PDFs. Web versions are also linked from the report home.

Disposition: unresolved renderer work. Editing diagram labels alone cannot provide PDF structure or alternative descriptions.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-2; the C4 content-verification record's PDF inspection.

C5-3: One Fusion sentence presents an older Palantir quarter as current

Reader impact: a sentence annualises the prior quarter at roughly USD 3.4 billion and describes 76% growth in the present tense. The refreshed report also carries the newer quarterly government revenue of USD 990 million and 79% growth.

Disposition: unresolved. The source chapter is frozen; correcting this sentence needs a specific owner exception and verification of every affected output. The issue was present before the refresh.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-3; segments/f4-fusion/chapter.md, the sentence beginning “runs at roughly a USD 3.4B annualized rate”.

C5-4: One Fusion sentence makes four vendors look like a formal tier

Reader impact: capitalised “Challengers (Siren, DataWalk, i2, Nuix)” suggests all four share that grade. Only Siren is graded Challenger.

Disposition: unresolved. This wording is in the frozen source chapter and needs an explicit exception before editing. The grading table remains the basis for each vendor's actual grade.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-4; segments/f4-fusion/chapter.md, the capitalised “Challengers” sentence.

C5-5: A landscape-editing script cannot currently run

Reader impact: this is a future maintenance risk. The script's expected text anchors are absent, so it stops before applying its changes.

Disposition: not run for this pack. Its dead widening step must be repaired or removed before the script is reused. The already rendered report assets are the inputs to this review set.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-5; gates/p4_landscapes_build.py and the C4 producer investigation.

C5-6: The Open-Source Intelligence reading deck has more diagrams than its chapter PDF

Reader impact: that source chapter references one of its five diagrams. Several diagram repairs therefore appear in the reading deck but not in the chapter PDF or full report.

Disposition: retained under the owner's instruction to record the difference without changing the frozen chapter. Use the Open-Source Intelligence reading deck to see the complete diagram set.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-6; C4 diagram and rendered-output comparison.

C5-7: Some pages have narrow-screen layout limitations

Reader impact: the earlier checks found elements wider than a 320-pixel screen in the companion, citations page and old index preview, even though ordinary page-overflow measurements reported no problem.

Disposition: the companion and citations limitations remain recorded. The replacement index preview uses the current Research-page layout and receives its own fresh mobile check; the old preview's result does not certify it. No report-wide layout repair is included in this hosting step.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-7; gates/c4-6-verification-ui-designer-2026-09-12.md and the C5 rendered review.

C5-8: Automated diagram checks do not detect every overlap

Reader impact: the automatic fit check cannot reliably detect all overlapping boxes. A small overlap in the Open-Source Intelligence timeline is among the recorded examples.

Disposition: the check is treated as a limited regression check. Rendered visual inspection remains necessary; a passing automated result does not establish that every diagram is visually correct.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-8; C4 diagram-check negative controls and gates/svg_panel_fit_check.js limitations.

C5-9: Two internal count checks still use the old tier label

Reader impact: this affects verification records rather than changing a vendor's displayed grade. Two configuration entries say “1 Wildcard” where the chapters now say “1 Challenger”.

Disposition: recorded for a scoped configuration correction. This pack does not treat the existing count check's exit code as proof of a clean report; its specific known failures must be distinguished from new ones.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-9; gates/count-invariants.json, entries C-f4-07 and C-f5-05.

C5-10: Small rendering defects and three citation URLs remain

Reader impact: the prior review recorded a run-together figure, an overlapping card pair and three citation URLs whose dash characters were altered.

Disposition: retained in the issue list. The prior comparison demonstrated these existed before the refresh. They are not silently repaired by restaging the files.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-10; the C4 rendered and content-verification records, including the backup comparison.

C5-11: Older verification tools have important limits

Reader impact: some earlier green results checked the August hosted set or compared a source with its own summary. Such results cannot establish that this review pack contains the September work.

Disposition: C5 uses checks bound to the actual staged and served files, including file identities and metadata in the browser's document head. This addresses acceptance of the review pack; it does not claim that every older checker has been repaired.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-11, and §13.14; the dated C5 staging, browser and served-file receipts.

C5-12: Historical working copies retain superseded figures

Reader impact: none within this pack. Earlier integration working directories contain historical copies and are outside the reader package.

Disposition: keep them as history. They must not be treated as current sources during a future refresh or added to the hosted review set.

Evidence: controlling plan §13.13.11, C5-12; sourcing/p1…p3-integration-working/.
