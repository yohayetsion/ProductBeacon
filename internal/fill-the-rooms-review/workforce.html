The Workforce

A workforce you govern. Here is everything it can do.

A workforce you govern should be one you can inspect. So the whole thing is on this page: every team, every specialist, and the work each one hands you. It arrives finished, in hours instead of weeks, and the decisions stay yours.

ROLE-BASED SPECIALISTS

Built around roles, not one-off tasks.

A generic task agent often starts with the immediate job and the prompt in front of it. Each ProductBeacon specialist starts with a defined professional role, then receives repeatable methods, domain references, relevant company context, and clear boundaries. Ask in business terms. The workforce routes the work to one specialist or, when the job crosses functions, a focused team. You receive one synthesis to review and decide.

From task agent to role-based specialist

FULL HARNESS

Every specialist arrives equipped for the role.

Before work starts, the harness searches for relevant company context, assigns the canonical role, and loads the methods and reference that match the task. The specialist also receives relevant boundaries and any triggered controls. When the work ends, the run declares what it loaded and what it produced. Human review still decides whether the work is good enough to use.

A fixed spawn, not an improvised prompt.

Every run follows a fixed core sequence: search for relevant context, establish the role, load the matching methods and reference, apply relevant boundaries and controls, and declare the handoff. Work that can create or change a Decision Record also checks relevant prior decisions before execution.

LOOP AND LEARN

The work is planned, checked, reviewed, and remembered.

You frame what good looks like. The workforce plans and executes in stages, fresh reviewers test the result, and you make the consequential calls. What is worth carrying forward is recorded so a later run can start with more of the right context.

Each cycle can leave the next one better prepared.

DISTRIBUTED COMPANY BRAIN

A company memory every specialist can work from.

Company knowledge is usually scattered across documents, chats, and the people who remember why a call was made. ProductBeacon keeps decisions, assumptions, feedback, learnings, bets, and deliverables as clearly labelled, inspectable records. Before the next run, relevant records are found and loaded into the specialist's focused context. You re-explain less, and you can correct the memory when reality changes.
