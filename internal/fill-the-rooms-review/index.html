The work of product leadership

The roadmap is only one part of the job.

The decisions that turn vision into value cross research, product, design, technology, go-to-market, finance, operations, people, risk, and delivery.

When the context fragments, decisions get remade, handoffs lose the intent, and accountability stops before the outcome. ProductBeacon keeps a named human at the centre while giving that person the methods, roles, evidence, and organisational memory to hold the whole loop.
